Chapter 8: Eliminate The Cause Of The Fight (Problem Solver 68 Vs Arius Squad)

The next day, Problem Solver 68 was still chasing after 'Sensei,' but still without much success.

Well, they weren't hacking into all the CCTVs, or eavesdropping on the police, or even deducing anything based on the traces Sensei left behind. Of course, the locations where they were active and where Sensei was hiding were almost close, but there was no way for them to know. Aru's spirit was gradually sinking due to the lack of progress.






It was like looking for a needle in a haystack in Seoul; their hatred for Sensei alone wasn't getting them anywhere. The driving force behind any ongoing task is a sense of accomplishment, but right now, only frustration is mounting, with no sense of achievement in sight.






"Boss, I think it's better to reconsider, even if it's now. Why don't we postpone finding Sensei for the time being and just take on requests again?"






Kayoko speaks realistically, but Aru's pride doesn't allow it.






"I can't stop now! If you draw your sword, you have to cut even a rotten radish!"






"Of course, I want to kill that guy just by looking at his face, but we can't find him this way. How are we going to find him if he's completely hidden? If we put out a missing person broadcast, will Sensei just walk out on his own?"






"Ugh... That's true but..."






When Aru becomes dejected, Mutsuki pats her on the back and comforts her.






"Don't be like that~ Kayoko-chan. You don't find a treasure chest by digging right away, do you? It takes time to catch a big shot~ And if we quit here, we might lose the chance, right? And most importantly, we have plenty of time since we're not getting any requests, right? It seems like we won't be getting any for a while either~ Kukuku."








"Y-Yeah. That's right! You can't expect to be full from the first bite! And if we achieve our goal after going through these hardships and difficulties, that's the path to becoming truly hard-boiled!"






"Indeed! So even though we're just hitting dead ends now, it will all have meaning later! Was this all part of Aru-sama's grand plan!"






"That's right! Ahaha..."






An instigator, a boastful one, and a blindly devoted follower combined created a scene that was touching from afar, but a tragedy up close.






'Sigh...'






Kayoko sighed to herself, but she didn't really stop them either. If she could have stopped them, most of the Problem Solver 68's baseless operations would have been cut short by Kayoko. She just follows along.






"Yeah, well, it's not like this is the first time we've done this. But we're not getting anywhere like this."






"That's true. Unless someone who knows where Sensei is fallen from the sky, we're stuck~ But that usually doesn't happen, so we have to move again, right? If we scour the Black Market district, something might come up, right? Shall we go there?"






As they were about to move, they heard a voice.






"Yes, Sensei. I'll be right there."






It was 'Sensei.' Aru clearly heard the word 'Sensei.' She immediately turned her head to see where the voice came from. There stood a woman holding a parasol. And that figure was somehow familiar.
