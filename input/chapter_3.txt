Chapter 3: The First Injury and Collaborator

Even late at night, the lights in Schale's office are still on. Sensei is there, busily typing away at the keyboard.






"Ugh... it never ends. How long do I have to keep doing this..."






Then, someone comes in.






"Sensei, what are you doing up so late?"






"What do you think, I'm working. Things have been crazy lately, it's a real pain. My workload has doubled."






"You're very busy. Right? Do you ever feel like you want to quit sometimes?"






"I do, but I can't. I want to rest, but what can I do? I'm the Sensei. I have to work hard for the students, don't I?"






"Ahaha."






BANG!






The sound of a gunshot echoes through the office, and Sensei's pure white uniform turns crimson red.






"Huh...?"






He clutches his stomach, but blood keeps flowing out from between his fingers. He can't muster any strength to stand up. In pain, Sensei struggles to turn his head and look to the side. And what he sees is a smile filled with pure malice.






"Who gave you permission to call yourself Sensei? We don't need someone like you."






"What are you talking about...?"






Click.






A gun is pointed at Sensei's head. But Sensei can't even resist.






"Just die quietly. It's for our sake, right?"






BANG!












"Ugh...!"






With a faint groan, Sensei opens his eyes. But his vision is blurry as if obscured by fog. And his mind is still not fully clear, he feels dizzy. His body also feels weak. But Sensei's brain is desperately telling his body to get up, that he must get up. He struggles to put strength into his body.






"Ughh...!"






The first place to regain strength is his mouth, and from there, strength gradually returns. From his mouth, it spreads to his eyes, from his eyes to his face, and then to his hands, arms, stomach, legs, and feet. And finally, his heart.






"Ughhh...!"






Energy spreads throughout his body. And now, Sensei is fully conscious and can see the world clearly.






".....Where is this?"






It's an unfamiliar place. Everything is unfamiliar. An unfamiliar ceiling, unfamiliar walls. Everything was dark. But the light coming in through the window was bright. The first thing Sensei thought when he saw this was:






'Am I still dreaming?'






Sensei tries to pinch his cheek with his hand. But the moment his hand comes into view, he realizes that it's not a dream.




"Huh.....?"






His left hand, with its ring and pinky fingers severed, no, crushed. The left hand was wrapped in white bandages. Where the fingers were missing, the bandages were soaked with blood. And as he realized this, a sharp pain shot through him. As he instinctively tried to reach for it with his right hand, Sensei felt severe pain in his right shoulder as well.
